---
title: "Research trends"
description: "Compare recent topic momentum and build an evidence-linked plan."
---

Trend Lab searches a topic, enriches a bounded sample of recent videos, and compares observable signals such as freshness, views, engagement, topic-relative velocity, and repeated creative patterns.

The report includes:

- Sample and methodology details
- Topic-level summaries and confidence
- Breakout, rising, and steady videos
- Visible hashtags, repeated title terms, and duration buckets
- Deterministic planning suggestions
- Optional model-generated themes and content gaps

The richer planning action validates the report before generating an angle, audience, hook, outline, titles, hashtags, differentiation, evidence, and caveats.

<Warning>
  Trend reports do not have access to private creator metrics such as click-through rate, retention, recommendation traffic, or proof of market demand. Early scans have lower confidence until snapshot history exists.
</Warning>
