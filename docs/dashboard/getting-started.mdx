---
title: "Dashboard quickstart"
description: "Search for a source, choose its data, and keep the useful context."
---

The dashboard is the visual workspace for video research. Sign in to keep projects and monitors tied to your account, or use the isolated demo identity when developing locally.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Sources">
    Enter a title, topic, YouTube video URL, or playlist URL. Search terms return reviewable video results; supported URLs open the matching source directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the additional data">
    Video details are always included. Select transcript, comments, channel information, or any combination before opening a video.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep the result useful">
    Save a source to a project, monitor its channel for new uploads, or use its timestamped evidence in your own API workflow.
  </Step>
</Steps>
